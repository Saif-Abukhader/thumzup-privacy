02

Common questions

How do I track my order?

Open the Orders tab in the app — you'll see it move from Placed → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered, and you'll get a push notification when it's ready and when it's out for delivery.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Danger zone → Delete account. You're signed out immediately, and your account is permanently erased 30 days later. Email us before then if you change your mind.

My payment didn't go through, or I was charged incorrectly.

Email us with your order details (approximate time and items ordered) and we'll look into it. For a CliQ transfer, include the CliQ note shown on your order — we check it against our bank. If an order was cancelled after you paid by CliQ, we send the money back.

Where does Thumzup deliver?

Kitchen No.3 currently delivers within Amman, Jordan.
