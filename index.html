01

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to take your order, get it to your door, and let you track it.

Account information

  • Full name, email, and phone number — collected when you create an account. Phone number is required so our kitchen and courier can reach you about your order.
  • Password — never stored by us in readable form; it's handled entirely by our authentication provider, Supabase.

Delivery & location

  • Delivery addresses you save or enter at checkout, either typed manually or filled in using your device's GPS location (only when you tap "Use current location").

Orders & payment

  • Order history — items, quantities, prices, promo codes used, and delivery status.
  • Payment — card payments are handled entirely on PayTabs' own hosted payment page. Your card number never reaches Thumzup's servers or database. If you choose cash on delivery, we only record that payment method against your order.

Notifications

  • Push notification token — a device identifier issued by Expo, used only to alert you when your order is ready or out for delivery.
02

How we use it

  • Take, prepare, and fulfill your orders
  • Get your order to the right address and keep you updated on its status (placed → preparing → out for delivery → delivered)
  • Send order-related push notifications (order ready, out for delivery)
  • Validate promo codes and prevent abuse
  • Maintain your account and order history so you can reorder easily
What we don't do We don't use your data for advertising, we don't run analytics or tracking SDKs in the app, and we never sell your personal information.

04

Retention & account deletion

You can request account deletion any time from Account → Danger zone → Delete account in the app.

30-day grace period When you request deletion, you're signed out immediately and your account is permanently erased 30 days later. Changed your mind? Contact us before the 30 days are up.

Order records themselves are kept after your account is deleted — with the link to your identity removed — since we're required to retain transaction records for accounting purposes. They're no longer associated with you.

05

Your choices

  • Location — you can decline location access; you'll just need to type your delivery address instead of using "Use current location."
  • Notifications — you can turn off push notifications in your device settings; you'll still see order status inside the app.
  • Addresses — add, edit, or remove saved delivery addresses any time from Account → Delivery addresses.
06

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth — we never see or store them directly.
  • Card numbers are entered directly on PayTabs' payment page and never pass through our servers.
  • Database access is restricted by row-level security so you can only see your own orders and addresses (kitchen staff can see order queues needed to fulfill them).
07

Children's privacy

Thumzup is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

08

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
